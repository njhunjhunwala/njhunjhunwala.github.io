Nivedita Jhunjhunwala

Ph.D. Candidate  ·  University of Texas at Austin  ·  Incoming Predoctoral Fellow, Stanford CISAC (2026–27)

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Government at the University of Texas at Austin, specializing in International Relations. My research examines how third-party states perceive international conflicts, the spread of misinformation during wartime, and the intersection of emerging technologies with international relations.

I am originally from Mumbai, India. I received my B.A. in Political Science and Global & International Studies, with a minor in Philosophy, from the State University of New York at Oswego in May 2021. I am currently a Graduate Fellow at the Clements Center.
